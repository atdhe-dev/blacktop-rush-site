Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Blacktop Rush ("the game") is made by OXA Developments ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

We do not collect or sell your personal information ourselves. The game has no accounts and no in-app purchases. It is free because it shows ads, provided by Google AdMob. AdMob collects some information from your device to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud, as described below.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When the game shows an ad, or loads one in the background, Google's Mobile Ads software in the game may collect and process:

  • Device identifiers: the advertising ID on Android, and on iPhone the advertising identifier (IDFA, only if you allow tracking) and an app-specific device identifier;
  • Your IP address, which can give an approximate (city-level) location;
  • Ad and app interaction data, such as which ads were shown or tapped and app launches;
  • Diagnostic data, such as crash, performance and device information (model, operating system, language).

Google uses this to show ads, to personalise them where you have allowed it, to measure how ads perform and to prevent fraud and abuse. Google is an independent controller of this data. Learn more in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google's Privacy Policy.

Your choices

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: when you first open the game, Google's consent message asks whether ads may use your data for personalised ads. You can change your answer any time under Pause → AD CHOICES.
  • iPhone: the game asks for permission to track (App Tracking Transparency). If you choose "Ask App Not to Track", the advertising identifier is not shared. You can change this in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Rewarded ads are optional: you only watch one if you tap "WATCH AD ×2".

If you refuse personalised ads, you will still see ads, but they will be less relevant to you.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, settings and a half-finished driveway) in a file on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This file never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the game deletes it.

Permissions

The game uses the internet to load ads. On Android it also uses vibration for haptic feedback (you can turn this off in the game's settings) and the advertising ID permission used by Google's ads software. The game does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or precise location.

Children

The game is made for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13. Ads in the game are limited to content suitable for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children; if you believe a child's information has been collected, contact us and we will help remove it.

Third parties

Besides Google AdMob, Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) handle downloads and may process data under their own privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
