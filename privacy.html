Privacy Policy

Last updated: 6 November 2026

Blacktop Rush ("the game") is made by OXA Developments ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to your information when you play.

The short version

We do not collect, store, share or sell any personal information. The game has no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases, and it does not connect to the internet.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress (unlocked levels, stars, coins, settings and a half-finished driveway) in a file on your own device so you can continue where you left off. This file never leaves your device, and we cannot see it. Deleting the game deletes it.

Permissions

On Android the game asks for permission to use vibration, for haptic feedback while you play. You can turn haptics off in the game's settings. The game does not use your camera, microphone, photos, contacts or location.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages. Because we collect no information from anyone, we collect none from children either.

Third parties

The game contains no third-party advertising, analytics or tracking software. Apple (App Store) and Google (Google Play) handle downloads and may process data under their own privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
