Quick answers

How do I play? Put your thumb anywhere on the screen and push: the tool follows. Keep the pace gauge in the green.

The sealer splashes onto the lawn. You're going too fast. Slow down until the gauge is green.

The concrete is scorching. Keep the heat gun moving; it only needs to dry the slab.

Vibration or sound off? Open the pause menu and toggle Haptics or Sound.

Will I lose my progress? Progress is saved on your phone automatically. Deleting the app deletes it.

Please include your phone model and what happened. Privacy: see our privacy policy.
